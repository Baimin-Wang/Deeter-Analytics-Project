# Note to the PM: broad, early gains

I would interpret "doing well quickly on many positions" as several independent position ideas showing early mark-to-market gains, rather than one large winner lifting the book. For an initial rule, count an idea when its side-adjusted price return, net of estimated round-trip trading costs and applicable accrued borrow, reaches +0.5% within 30 minutes of its first fill. Price return uses entry notional, not an undefined "risk capital" denominator. The 0.5% cutoff is a working noise buffer, not a claim that it is suitable for every instrument; 30 minutes captures the first half-hour after entry. Both need replay and PM feedback.

At each check, the eligible cohort is currently open ideas aged at most 30 minutes with fresh marks. "Many" means at least max(5, ceiling(50% of that cohort)) qualifying ideas, and the cohort's median net return must be positive. Five is a breadth floor; half the cohort prevents five winners in a much larger losing cohort from triggering. Fewer than five eligible ideas means no alert. Aggregate partial fills by idea, use a weighted entry price, and do not reset the clock on an add; a fully closed and reopened idea starts a new clock. Exclude older holdings from this early-gain cohort.

Check once a minute, using marks no older than 60 seconds. Require the condition at two consecutive checks to suppress one-mark spikes. Fire only on entry into the alert state, with a 30-minute minimum between alerts; re-arm only after five consecutive failed checks. No separate recovery notification. These operational cutoffs are proposed defaults, not measured false-positive optima.

Include qualifying/eligible counts, median and aggregate net P&L, holding ages, contributors and sector concentration. Flag broad market alignment alongside the result; do not silently subtract an unvalidated beta model. Suppress the alert when fewer than 90% of otherwise eligible ideas have valid fresh marks, and report the data issue separately. Replay timestamped fills, costs and quotes before enabling it; review triggers with the PM to distinguish useful breadth from correlated market moves. This note proposes an alert, not an instruction to add risk.
